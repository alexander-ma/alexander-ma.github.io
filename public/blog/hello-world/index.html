A new beginning

Why I am making this site and what I plan to share here.
September 9, 2026 · 1 min · Alex Ma

A place to think in public#

I am starting this site as a home for my work and a record of what I learn while making things.

The portfolio will grow over time. For now, this first version is deliberately simple: a clear introduction, a place for notes, and an open invitation to connect.

I will use this space to share project write-ups, experiments, useful references, and the occasional lesson that only becomes obvious after doing the work.
