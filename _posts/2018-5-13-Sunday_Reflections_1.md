# What did you do this past week?
This past week was pretty stressful with finals coming around to round out the spring semester. I've always had trouble managing my time when it comes to studying for two finals on the same day, but I think I did alright on both of those exams (but I definitely could've done better). After taking two finals on Thursday, I finally had a chance to breathe and relax a bit from the stress that school puts on me.

I'm still actively searching for internships over the summer, and I've been beating myself up about not being about to find anything having both the fall and spring semester to search for one. In reality, I should be progressing my career at my own pace, and if I don't feel ready to search for an internship, I shouldn't be looking until I feel prepared to look for one. There's a lot of brushing up that I have to do in terms of studying for technical interviews, reviewing core fundamental programming knowledge, and getting to know myself better and building that confidence to talk about myself without bringing myself down.

# What did you learn?
One thing that really hit me this week was when my roommate and I had a personal conversation about our feelings and emotional situation. There's been a lot on my mind about where I am right now, and whether or not I'm happy with where I see myself in the near future. Long story short, I'm starting to think more about who I am, and I'm slowly learning more about the person I'm growing up to be, and a bit more about who I am as of right now (This was a bit convoluted and confusing, but please bare with me LOL).

# What's in your way?
I currently have my Jazz Appreciation final on tuesday (the very last day of finals week wtf) and haven't put much effort into studying for it yet. I'm a pretty avid jazz guy, and I feel as though this exam will be pretty easy and straightforward - nothing like Concurrent and Distributed Systems or Vector Calculus.

I've also found that I've been holding myself back to things that I want to pursue because I sometimes don't have the balls to do it. I've been thinking about starting to get serious about photography and buying a DSLR camera, but I've been really holding myself back from it due to the costs and whether I'll have the time to actively continue with taking pictures. We'll see where this goes.
