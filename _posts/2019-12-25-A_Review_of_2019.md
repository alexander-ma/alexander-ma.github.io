It's crazy to look back and think about all of the things that has happened in my life so far. In 2009, I was in middle school. No one likes middle school. Thankfully a lot has changed since the beginning of the decade, but this year has been action-packed. So...

## What happened this year?

The 2019 year alone has been a wild ride, so hopefully I can get a chance to cover a good amount of what went down.

### I finished school!

I graduated from The University of Texas at Austin with a B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering! So much of this decade has been me dreaming for this moment to happen, and now I'm left asking myself what else is next in my life. College was an experience that I'd never trade for anything. I'm super grateful for the friends that I've made during my four years at UT, the brutal anxieties and pressures from my classes and workload, and disappointing amount of rejections that came from finding a job as a new grad after school.

### I found a job!

Working as a software engineer at JPMorgan Chase & Co. has been an a ton of fun where I can get to learn and build upon my skillset. I'm surrounded by such brilliant people that have been at the firm as long as I have, as well as people that have been around for the entire decade. I'm also grateful to be around people that are willing to help me grow and kick start my career fresh out of college. There are so many more things to achieve in my professional life, and I can't wait to see where it gets me.

### I live alone!

I've never had a chance to have my own sense of freedom and the feeling of independence of living by myself without any roommates. Having a roommate in college was a necessity in order to save money on rent. Now having this opportunity to have my own space, I've noticed that my mental health has improved a lot, and I've been able to really concentrate on things that are important to me. Paying a bit more for rent may suck at times, but freedom and peace of mind that I get is well worth it in my opinion.

## What did you learn?

### The Art of Being Alone

One of the most important things I've learned so far is the ability to embrace loneliness.

Having the freedom to live alone adds to this a bit, but there are days where I still don't feel to myself because of one thing: the internet.

Everyone nowadays has a cell phone, and most likely has a means of using the internet to reach anyone they want around the world. The thought that someone can just message me in the matter of seconds about anything can be nice and refreshing sometimes, but it could also be a major distraction.

Being able to detach myself from things that connect me to other such as social media and my cell phone helps me enjoy the time that I truly get to myself. I still catch myself mindlessly scrolling on Twitter and striking countless conversations with people on Facebook Messenger, but the feeling of being in complete isolation puts me in an elevated state of mind of getting a deeper understanding of myself and embracing discomfort.

## What's next?

I have a tendency of wanting to do a lot of things, but never doing them. This includes:

- Reading more
- Traveling more
- Writing more (hopefully)
- Focusing more on my health
- Learning new things
- Finding new hobbies/passions

These are just things off the top of my head, but it's probably better to have a more concise list of goals for the next year.

### Read a book a month

I've been reading James Clear's "Atomic Habits" for the past couple of months, and it has completely changed  the way I see myself and the processes that are unconsciously happening without me knowing. For anyone looking to develop better habits and breaking the bad ones, I definitely suggest reading this!

I've been very attracted towards non-fiction self-improvement type books lately, so I feel like I should change it up and read something a bit different. There's been a lot of book lists that have been releasing since the new year is coming around, so I'm have a lot to pick and choose from. I want to read a fiction book next, so I might pick up "Where the Crawdads Sing" by Delia Owens or "Little Fires Everywhere" by Celeste Ng.

### Traveling more

For several years, I've been pretty envious of my friend Max's travel adventures on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/waxmang/). I've also grown a bit tired of always going back home for the holidays, so I figured that I won't be accomplishing much if I'm just daydreaming about all the places I could go, when I could just go and do it.

I've never planned a trip before, and I'm not a very spontaneous person to just pack my bags and head into unknown territory. The danger just makes it seem twice as fun though. I told myself that I would go on a solo road trip to Colorado or New Mexico to try out skiing, but I'll update if that actually happens. 😅

## See you around soon...?

Thanks for reading, and I hope to write some more over this upcoming year. No promises though. Goals are meant to be broken.

Hope you've had an amazing year along with me, and it's time to finish up the year strong. Happy New Year, and much love.
